Profile

Design shaped by
more than design.

I am a designer and builder interested in how brands shape attention, expectation and behavior.

Six years spent living in Myanmar and Sri Lanka formed my eye for symbolism, restraint and systems of meaning. My technical work taught me to turn those ideas into functioning digital products.

I study how images, language and systems influence what people notice, remember, trust and act upon. The goal is not decoration, but the form that makes an idea feel inevitable.
